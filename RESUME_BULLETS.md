# Resume bullets: Fixed-Income Risk Engine

Each number is generated by `python -m firisk.run_all` (simulated portfolio, not real AUM).

* Built a from-scratch Python fixed-income pricing and risk engine (no QuantLib wrapping) covering ACT/ACT and 30/360
  accrual, coupon schedules, clean/dirty pricing, YTM, Treasury curve bootstrapping (par instruments reprice to
  1.4e-14), spot/forward/discount curves, Macaulay/modified/effective duration, convexity, DV01, 11-bucket
  key-rate DV01, z-spread, spread duration and CS01.
* Analyzed a simulated $100.00MM Treasury/IG-credit portfolio (32 bonds, 13 issuers, 8
  sectors) against a simulated 60/40 benchmark: duration 7.23 vs 5.18, DV01 $72,277/bp with the largest
  key-rate bucket at 10Y ($17,614/bp), CS01 $49,092/bp. Key-rate buckets reconcile to parallel DV01
  within -0.00003%.
* Ran 11 full-repricing stress scenarios (parallel ±25/50/100bp, steepener/flattener, tiered IG widening, combined
  rates+credit), with a worst case of -$9.17MM (-9.17% NAV). Showed that convexity-adjusted
  approximations cut the worst error from 8.72% to 0.86% of P&L, and verified 2nd/3rd-order error
  convergence (fitted log-log slopes 1.99 / 3.00).
* Ran PCA on 2,687 days of FRED Treasury curve changes and identified level/slope/curvature empirically
  (85.1%/11.0%/2.1% of variance). Mapped them onto key-rate DV01s for factor risk: PC1 is 94.5% of
  portfolio rate variance, parametric 99% 1-day VaR is $849,250 and historical VaR is $960,859.
* Built a HiGHS linear-programming rebalancer with issuer/sector caps, a minimum Treasury allocation, a turnover limit
  and transaction costs. It cleared 3 policy breaches, moved duration 7.23→5.30 and cut
  the key-rate tracking gap 88% at 19.99% turnover and 1.47bp cost, with every constraint
  verified independently after trade rounding.
* Shipped with 55 pytest test functions against closed-form fixtures (zero-coupon identity, par-at-par, annuity pricing,
  analytic-vs-finite-difference DV01, accrual edge cases), an optional QuantLib cross-check, CI, pinned dependencies,
  an offline data cache, and README/memo numbers regenerated from program output.
